## What is ify?

ify is Resolution AI for support teams: an AI agent that resolves tickets end to end on top of Freshdesk, Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot or Intercom, billed per verified resolution.

HubSpot refers to Service Hub. ify serves support teams across small businesses, mid-market companies and enterprises, without requiring a helpdesk migration.

## Connect knowledge and approved actions

ify builds knowledge from documentation, websites, release notes, video walkthroughs and past resolved tickets. It creates missing SOPs and refreshes connected sources on a schedule.

Supported actions through appropriate connected apps include refunds, trial extensions, CRM field and account updates, supported order changes, payout re-triggers, and ticket or Jira issue creation. Availability depends on your integrations, permissions and workflow.

## Keep your team involved

- **Autonomous:** Handle eligible requests within configured instructions and guardrails.
- **Human-in-the-loop:** Include human involvement where the workflow requires it.
- **AI Assist:** Keep your team involved while ify assists with support work.

Configure the operating mode by ticket type. ify supports handoff with context and an approval trail.
