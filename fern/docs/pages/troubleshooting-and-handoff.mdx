Use these checklists to narrow down a problem before you escalate it. If a checklist doesn't resolve the issue, ask your ify admin to review the configuration.

## Missing knowledge

ify can't find an answer you expect it to know.

- [ ] Is the knowledge source connected?
- [ ] Has a sync run since the content was added or updated?
- [ ] Is the content in a supported format?
- [ ] Does an SOP exist for this type of request?

If the issue persists, ask your admin to review your knowledge sources. See [Knowledge setup](/knowledge-setup).

## Unavailable action

ify doesn't perform, or doesn't propose, an action you expect.

- [ ] Is the app the action depends on connected?
- [ ] Does the action match a supported action category?
- [ ] Are guardrails blocking the action for this ticket type?

If the issue persists, ask your admin to review your connected apps and guardrails. See [Actions and approvals](/actions-and-approvals).

## Permission errors

ify can't access a connected app or complete an action because of access issues.

- [ ] Does ify have the necessary access to the connected app?
- [ ] Has the connection been re-authorised recently?

If the issue persists, ask your admin to review ify's access to the connected app.

## Handoff problems

A ticket isn't handed off as expected, or the receiving agent is missing information.

- [ ] Are handoff rules configured for this ticket type?
- [ ] Is the conversation context being passed to the receiving agent?
- [ ] Is the approval trail visible to the receiving agent?

If the issue persists, ask your admin to review your handoff rules.
