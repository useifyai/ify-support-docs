ify draws on your existing knowledge to understand customer issues and resolve tickets. The more complete and current your sources are, the better ify can answer.

## Supported knowledge sources

| Source | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Documentation | Help center articles, product guides |
| Website content | Public pages on your website |
| Release notes | Product updates and changelogs |
| Video walkthroughs | Recorded product demos and how-to videos |
| Past resolved tickets | Tickets your team has already resolved |

## SOPs

ify creates missing SOPs automatically from your existing knowledge. Review generated SOPs with your team to confirm they reflect how you want tickets handled.

## Scheduled refresh

Connected sources re-sync on a schedule, so updates to your content reach ify without manual re-uploads.
