ify works on top of your existing helpdesk — no migration required. ify supports the five helpdesks below.

<Note>
Helpdesk integrations are separate from the apps ify uses to take business actions, such as billing and payments. See [Actions and approvals](/actions-and-approvals).
</Note>

## Zendesk

Zendesk is a customer service platform for managing support tickets across channels.

- **Integration details:** [ify for Zendesk](https://useify.ai/integrations/zendesk)
Before connecting, review the required plan, account permissions and prerequisites with your helpdesk administrator.

## Freshdesk

Freshdesk is a customer support platform for managing tickets and support workflows.

- **Integration details:** [ify for Freshdesk](https://useify.ai/integrations/freshdesk)
Before connecting, review the required plan, account permissions and prerequisites with your helpdesk administrator.

## Salesforce

Salesforce is a CRM platform with customer service and case management capabilities.

- **Integration details:** [ify for Salesforce](https://useify.ai/integrations/salesforce)
Before connecting, review the required plan, account permissions and prerequisites with your helpdesk administrator.

## HubSpot (Service Hub)

HubSpot Service Hub is HubSpot's customer service software for managing support tickets.

- **Integration details:** [ify for HubSpot](https://useify.ai/integrations/hubspot)
Before connecting, review the required plan, account permissions and prerequisites with your helpdesk administrator.

Intercom is a customer communications platform for messaging and support.

Before connecting, review the required plan, account permissions and prerequisites with your helpdesk administrator.
